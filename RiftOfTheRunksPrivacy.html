We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties for their marketing purposes. Any third-party access to information is strictly limited to the purposes stated above.

 

Automatic Data Collection and Advertising

Firebase Analytics

Our app uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC, to collect and analyze usage data. Firebase Analytics helps us understand how users interact with our app, including which features are used, how long sessions last, and other relevant information. This data is collected in aggregate and is used to improve the user experience and performance of the app.

  • Data Collected: Firebase Analytics collects information such as device identifiers, app usage data, and interaction events. It may also collect location data if permitted by the user.
  • Purpose: The information gathered is used to improve app performance, detect crashes, and enhance user experience.
  • Data Sharing: The data collected by Firebase Analytics is shared with Google. You can review Google's Privacy Policy for more details: Google Privacy Policy.
Yandex AppMetrica

We also use Yandex AppMetrica, a service provided by Yandex LLC, to gather statistical and diagnostic information about how the app is used. AppMetrica provides insights into app performance, user behavior, and technical issues to help us continuously improve the app.

  • Data Collected: AppMetrica collects data such as device information, app usage statistics, geographic location, and technical logs.
  • Purpose: This data is used for app analytics, performance monitoring, and diagnosing errors or crashes. It helps us identify issues and enhance the overall user experience.
  • Data Sharing: The data collected by Yandex AppMetrica is stored and processed by Yandex. You can read more about Yandex's Privacy Policy here: Yandex Privacy Policy.
